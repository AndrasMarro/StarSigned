Mythology

The sea-goat: half goat, half fish. In Greek myth the god Pan leapt into a river to escape the monster Typhon and changed shape halfway. The figure is far older, going back to Babylon.

In the night sky

One of the faintest zodiac constellations, shaped like a wide smile or arrowhead. Its brightest star is Deneb Algedi, “the tail of the goat”.

Did you know?

The Tropic of Capricorn got its name the same way as the Tropic of Cancer: the Sun stood in Capricorn at the December solstice in antiquity.
