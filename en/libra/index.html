Mythology

The scales of justice held by Astraea, the neighbouring Virgo. The Greeks first saw these stars as the claws of the Scorpion before the Romans made them a sign of their own.

In the night sky

Its two brightest stars still carry the old names Zubenelgenubi and Zubeneschamali, Arabic for “the southern claw” and “the northern claw”.

Did you know?

Libra is the only sign of the zodiac represented by an object rather than a person or an animal.
