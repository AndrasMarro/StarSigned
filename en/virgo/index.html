Nice to know

How the zodiac works →

Mythology

Often linked to Astraea, goddess of justice and the last immortal to leave Earth at the end of the Golden Age. She is also seen as Demeter or Persephone holding a sheaf of wheat.

In the night sky

Spica, the “ear of wheat” in her hand, is one of the brightest stars in the sky. Virgo also holds the Virgo Cluster, a group of more than 1,000 galaxies.

Did you know?

Virgo is the largest constellation of the zodiac and the second largest of all 88. The Sun spends about 45 days crossing it, far longer than one sign.
