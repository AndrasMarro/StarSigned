Nice to know

How the zodiac works →

Mythology

Hera sent a giant crab to distract Heracles while he fought the many-headed Hydra. He crushed it underfoot, and Hera placed it among the stars for its loyalty.

In the night sky

The faintest constellation of the zodiac. At its heart lies the Beehive Cluster (M44), a swarm of stars visible to the naked eye on dark nights.

Did you know?

The Tropic of Cancer is named after it, because the Sun stood in Cancer at the June solstice when the name was given, about 2,000 years ago.
