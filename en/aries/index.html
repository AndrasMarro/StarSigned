Mythology

Aries is Chrysomallos, the winged ram with a golden fleece that rescued the children Phrixus and Helle. Its fleece later became the prize Jason and the Argonauts sailed to find.

In the night sky

A small constellation whose brightest star is Hamal, an orange giant about 66 light-years away. Its name comes from the Arabic for “the head of the ram”.

Did you know?

About 2,000 years ago the spring equinox lay in Aries, so astrologers still call it the “First Point of Aries”. Because of precession it has since drifted into Pisces.
