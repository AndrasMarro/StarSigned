Nice to know

How the zodiac works →

Mythology

Ganymede, a beautiful Trojan youth carried to Olympus by Zeus in the form of an eagle to become the cupbearer of the gods.

In the night sky

Its brightest stars are Sadalsuud and Sadalmelik, “luck of lucks” and “luck of the king”. Several of its stars form the Water Jar.

Did you know?

The Eta Aquariids meteor shower each May is made of dust left behind by Halley’s Comet. The “Age of Aquarius” refers to the spring equinox slowly moving into this constellation.
