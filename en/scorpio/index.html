A closer look

Element

Water

Water is emotion, intuition and depth. Water signs feel everything and read between the lines. They heal and protect, but can be overwhelmed by the tides of their own moods.

Shared with: ♋︎ Cancer, ♓︎ Pisces

Modality

Fixed

Fixed signs sit in the middle of a season, when it is at its strongest. They stabilise and persevere: loyal, focused and hard to move once decided.

Season: Height of autumn in the northern hemisphere.

Shared with: ♉︎ Taurus, ♌︎ Leo, ♒︎ Aquarius

Ruling planet

Pluto

Pluto stands for transformation, power and rebirth. It rules what lies hidden and what must end to begin again. Traditional co-ruler: Mars.

House

8th house

The house of transformation: intimacy, shared resources, inheritance, crisis and rebirth.

In the natural zodiac each sign is linked to one of the twelve houses, the areas of life in a birth chart.

Body

Reproductive system

Medical astrology maps the zodiac onto the body, from Aries at the head down to Pisces at the feet. Scorpio is linked to the reproductive system: traditionally a source of strength and an area that benefits from extra care.

Polarity

Yin

Yin: receptive, inward and reflective. Earth and water signs gather energy and work from the inside out.

Nice to know

How the zodiac works →

Mythology

The scorpion that stung the boastful hunter Orion. The gods placed them on opposite sides of the sky, so Orion sets in the west as Scorpius rises in the east.

In the night sky

Antares, its red heart, is a supergiant roughly 700 times wider than the Sun. Its name means “rival of Mars” because of its colour.

Did you know?

Astronomically the Sun spends less than ten days in Scorpius, then about 18 days in Ophiuchus, the “13th constellation” that the zodiac signs leave out.
