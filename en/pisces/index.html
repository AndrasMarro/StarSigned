Nice to know

Mythology

Aphrodite and her son Eros turned into fish and tied themselves together with a cord to escape Typhon, so they would not lose each other in the river.

In the night sky

A faint, sprawling constellation. Its stars trace two fish joined by a long ribbon, knotted at the star Alrescha, “the cord”.

Did you know?

The spring equinox point, where the Sun crosses the celestial equator each March, now lies in Pisces. This is the reference point of the tropical zodiac.
