Nice to know

Mythology

Castor and Pollux, the inseparable twins. When mortal Castor died, Pollux begged Zeus to share his immortality, so the brothers now alternate between Olympus and the underworld.

In the night sky

The twin stars Castor and Pollux mark their heads. Castor looks like one star but is actually a system of six stars orbiting each other.

Did you know?

Every December the Geminids, one of the richest meteor showers of the year, appear to radiate from this constellation.
