Mythology

Zeus disguised himself as a gleaming white bull to win Europa, carrying her across the sea to Crete. The continent of Europe is named after her.

In the night sky

Home of Aldebaran, the red “eye of the bull”, and of two famous star clusters: the Hyades forming its face and the Pleiades, the Seven Sisters, on its shoulder.

Did you know?

The Crab Nebula sits near the bull’s horn. It is the remnant of a supernova that Chinese astronomers recorded in the year 1054, bright enough to see in daylight.
