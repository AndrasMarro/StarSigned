Mythology

The Nemean Lion, whose golden hide no weapon could pierce. Slaying it was the first of the twelve labours of Heracles, who wore its skin as armour ever after.

In the night sky

Its brightest star Regulus, “the little king”, sits almost exactly on the ecliptic, so the Moon and planets regularly pass close by it.

Did you know?

The Leonids meteor shower peaks every November. In some years, such as 1833 and 1966, it produced storms of thousands of meteors per hour.
